## Overview

**Sahara** is a web-based e-commerce platform that facilitates online retail transactions. It handles product catalog presentation, dynamic shopping carts, order placement, and back-office store administration.

## Core Features

- **Product Discovery & Browsing**: Hierarchical product categories, item details, pricing, and stock indicators.
- **Cart & Checkout**: Real-time item quantity adjustment, subtotal calculations, and order confirmation workflows.
- **Order Management**: End-to-end tracking for completed purchases and shipping statuses.
- **Seller & Admin Dashboards**: Administrative portals for product creation, stock adjustments, and account moderation.
- **Relational Backend**: Structured MySQL schema ensuring data consistency across customers, products, orders, and order items.
